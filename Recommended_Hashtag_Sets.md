# Recommended Hashtag Sets – Horizon Digital

**Author:** Reddy Malli Nandini  
**Last Updated:** October 2026

These sets are pre-built for common content pillars. Copy, paste, and lightly customize based on the exact post topic.

**Platform Rules (2026):**
- **Instagram:** Maximum 5 hashtags. Aim for 3–5 highly relevant tags.
- **LinkedIn:** 2–3 hashtags optimal. Place at the end of the post.

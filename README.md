## Project Overview

This repository contains a complete **Hashtag Strategy** developed for the brand **Horizon Digital** – a digital marketing agency focused on helping startups and SMBs grow through social media, content marketing, and performance strategies.

The strategy follows a structured approach by categorizing hashtags into:

- **Branded**
- **Niche**
- **Industry**
- **Community**
- **Campaign**

### Objective
Learn structured hashtag research and improve social discoverability on Instagram and LinkedIn.

### Tools Used
- Instagram (search + Insights concepts)
- LinkedIn
- Google Search
- Best-practice research from 2026 industry sources

## How to Use This Strategy

1. Open the Excel research sheet and review the categorized hashtags.
2. Choose a content type (Educational, Case Study, BTS, etc.).
3. Copy the recommended set for Instagram or LinkedIn.
4. Always include at least one **branded** hashtag.
5. Track performance monthly and refine.

### Key Principles Applied
- Prioritize relevance over popularity
- Do not rely only on extremely popular hashtags
- Mix categories strategically (1 branded + 1–2 niche + optional industry/community/campaign)
- Respect 2026 platform limits: Instagram ≤ 5, LinkedIn 2–3
